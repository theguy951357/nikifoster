About Me

I’m an attorney dedicated to making legal services more accessible, affordable, and empowering especially for those navigating the system alone. Through my unbundled law practice, I provide limited scope representation tailored to the specific needs of clients who prefer more control over their legal journey.

Whether you're facing a divorce, landlord dispute, custody issue, or small claims matter, you shouldn't have to choose between going it completely alone or spending thousands on full legal representation. That's why I offer Pro Se support — step-by-step legal guidance designed to help unrepresented parties advocate for themselves effectively and confidently.

My goal is simple: to help people feel equipped, not overwhelmed. I’ve worked with countless individuals who just needed the right tools, advice, and confidence to present their case. With unbundled legal services, I can support you where it matters most — drafting documents, preparing you for court, or reviewing agreements — while you stay in control of your case and your budget.

If you're looking for an attorney who understands the power of strategic, affordable, and limited legal help, I invite you to reach out. Together, we can make sure your voice is heard — whether you're working Pro Se or just need help with specific parts of your legal matter.
