About

I’m Niki Foster, an attorney dedicated to making legal services more accessible and empowering for those navigating the civil legal system alone. My practice centers on unbundled legal services and limited scope representation, designed specifically to support pro se litigants.

I assist unrepresented individuals behind the scenes throughout North Carolina. That includes drafting motions, pleadings, discovery responses, and participating in settlement negotiations, all without appearing as counsel of record.

Whether you’re involved in a divorce, housing dispute, custody matter, or small claims case, my goal is to help you feel equipped and informed, not overwhelmed. You can get high-quality legal guidance without having to pay a large retainer.

I offer legal help on a flat-fee, case-by-case basis with no upfront commitment. The initial consultation is free, and we’ll determine what level of support best fits your needs.

Legal Services

I offer strategic assistance in areas including family law, housing, contracts, and small claims. My limited scope representation model allows you to choose exactly the support you need, when you need it most.
